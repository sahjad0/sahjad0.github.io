❤️ 36 Questions That Lead to Love ❤️

Developed by Psychologist Arthur Aron
Answer each question honestly and take turns with your partner.

💖 Set I

1. If you could invite anyone to dinner, who would you choose?
2. Would you like to be famous? Why?
3. Do you rehearse what you'll say before making a phone call?
4. What would make a perfect day for you?
5. When did you last sing to yourself? To someone else?
6. If you could live to 90, would you rather keep the body of a 30-year-old or the mind of a 30-year-old?
7. Do you have a feeling about how you might die?
8. Name three things you and your partner seem to have in common.
9. What in your life makes you feel most grateful?
10. If you could change something about how you were raised, what would it be?
11. Tell your life story in about four minutes.
12. If you could wake up tomorrow with one new ability or quality, what would it be?

💜 Set II

13. If a crystal ball could reveal one truth about your future or yourself, what would you want to know?
14. Is there something you've dreamed of doing but haven't? Why not?
15. What achievement are you most proud of?
16. What do you value most in a friendship?
17. What's your favorite memory?
18. What's your most painful memory?
19. If you knew you had only one year left to live, would you change how you live?
20. What does friendship mean to you?
21. What roles do love and affection play in your life?
22. Take turns mentioning five positive qualities you see in each other.
23. How close and supportive is your family?
24. How do you feel about your relationship with your mother?

❤️ Set III

25. Make three true "we" statements about yourselves.
26. Complete: "I wish I had someone to share..."
27. If you were to become close friends, what should they know about you?
28. Tell your partner something you genuinely appreciate about them.
29. Share an embarrassing moment from your life.
30. When did you last cry by yourself? With someone else?
31. What do you already like about your partner?
32. What topic is too serious to joke about?
33. If you died tonight without being able to contact anyone, what would you regret not having said? Why?
34. If your home caught fire and everyone was safe, what one item would you try to save?
35. Whose death in your family would affect you the most? Why?
36. Share a personal problem and ask your partner how they would handle it. Also ask what feelings they think you're experiencing.
❤️ After completing all 36 questions, spend 4 minutes maintaining uninterrupted eye contact with each other.
